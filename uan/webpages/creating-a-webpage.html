AI for Educators

Turn your lesson ideas into interactive webpages without writing a single line of code.

Think of it like this: You are the Head Chef (the subject matter expert), and the AI is your lightning-fast Sous-Chef. You don't need to learn how to code today; you just need to practice writing a great recipe!

The 5-Step Workflow

Phase 1

The Hook & Demystification

Don't panic! We aren't learning HTML today. AI tools like Claude (using Artifacts) or v0.dev have turned web design into a simple conversation. Your job is simply to be a clear communicator.

Phase 2

Define the Educational Goal

Start small. Don't build an entire course. Pick one of these three options to build today:

  • A 5-question interactive quiz.
  • A set of digital flashcards.
  • A drag-and-drop matching game.

Action: Take 10 minutes to write your questions, answers, or terms on a piece of paper.

Phase 4

Generate and Tweak

Paste your prompt into your AI tool. If it's not perfect on the first try, just tell the AI what to fix, exactly like talking to an assistant! (e.g., "Make the text larger" or "Make the background blue").

Phase 5

Publish and Share

Once you are happy, click the "Share" or "Publish" button in your AI tool to get a link. You can immediately email this link to your students or post it in your learning management system.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • Expect things to break: If the AI generates a weird glitch, embrace it! Just type: "There is a bug, the [element] isn't working correctly. Please fix it."
  • Don't refresh: Keep all your requests in the same chat thread so the AI remembers the context of your lesson.
  • Ask for options: If you don't like the colors, say: "I don't love the colors. Give me 3 different color palette options to choose from."
